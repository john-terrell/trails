# Augmenting Human Intellect

The domain above [Personal Knowledge Management](personal-knowledge-management.md): how tools, language, method and
training combine to raise what a person can actually comprehend and solve. Coined as a
research programme by [Douglas Engelbart](douglas-engelbart.md) in
[1962](Sources.md), who defined it as *"increasing the
capability of a man to approach a complex problem situation, to gain comprehension to
suit his particular needs, and to derive solutions to problems."* PKM is one
specialization — the record-keeping and retrieval corner. This hub covers the whole.

## Current understanding

**The unit of analysis is the system.** This is Engelbart's central reframe and the
reason this hub exists separately from PKM. Effectiveness is not a property of a person,
a tool, or a method, but of [H-LAM/T](h-lam-t.md) — all four together. *"Individuals who operate
effectively in our culture have already been considerably 'augmented.'"* Everything else
follows: you cannot evaluate a note-taking app in isolation, because the app is one
component of a system that includes the user's language, their methodology, and their
training. A vault that changes the artifact but not the method changes very little —
which is why Engelbart insists *"even if the equipment appeared on the market tomorrow, a
good deal of empirical research would be needed to develop a methodology."*

**Intelligence is organization.** Chasing the "source of intelligence" down through a
computer's layers terminates not in a substance but in structure: *"If there is any one
thing upon which this 'intelligence' depends, it would seem to be organization."* Hence
[Synergism](synergism.md), and hence [Intelligence Amplification](intelligence-amplification.md) — the amplified intelligence
belongs to the combined system, not to the human inside it. This reframes what an LLM
agent in a knowledge base *is*: not a smart tool used by a person, but a component of a
system whose intelligence exceeds either part.

**Serial media misrepresent networked thought.** Concept structures are n-dimensional and
multiply-linked; paper, prose and markdown are one-dimensional. Engelbart: *"it is like
trying to project n-dimensional forms… onto a one-dimensional form (the serial string of
symbols), where the human memory and visualization has to hold and picture the links and
relationships."* [N-Dimensional Projection Problem](n-dimensional-projection-problem.md) is a standing critique of this
vault's medium, not a problem it has solved. Hubs and graph view are compensation.

**Structure types constrain each other.** *"Process structuring limiting symbol
structuring, symbol structuring limiting concept structuring, and concept structuring
limiting mental structuring."* A wiki is a symbol-structuring system; its limits
propagate upward into what its owner can think with it. That is the strongest argument in
the corpus for taking notation seriously — and for [Typed Links](typed-links.md), which this vault
adopted on Engelbart's evidence.

**The regenerative strategy.** Engelbart's research plan turns on augmentation
researchers augmenting themselves: *"their job assignment is to develop means that will
make them more effective at doing their job."* [Regenerative Feature](regenerative-feature.md) describes this
vault exactly — a knowledge base about knowledge bases, maintained by the method it
documents, where each ingest improves the tool doing the ingesting.

**How firmly to hold this.** One framework document and one essay, both proposals, neither
an experiment. Engelbart's most persuasive material — the card system's failure — is
first-person but n=1. His most vivid material (§III.B) is fiction. The corpus has still
never measured anything, and Engelbart himself only asks for two things: knowing *when*
an improvement happened, and being able to *compare* two competing ones. This vault
currently has neither.

## Tensions and open questions

- **Is markdown the wrong medium?** [N-Dimensional Projection Problem](n-dimensional-projection-problem.md) says a serial
  text wiki systematically misrepresents concept structure. The vault's answer so far —
  hubs, graph view, typed relations — is compensation, not solution.
- **Typed links: adopted on qualitative evidence.** Engelbart reports cleaner structures
  and fewer members, with no numbers. We adopted the practice anyway. Worth reviewing
  once enough typed links exist to judge. → Open Questions
- **Conflicts that resolve to "neither."** Engelbart allows an argument to bear out
  *"one, or the other, or neither stand."* Contradictions has no such outcome.
- **The lineage stops at 1962.** Bush → list processing → Engelbart is sourced.
  Engelbart → hypertext → the web is not.
